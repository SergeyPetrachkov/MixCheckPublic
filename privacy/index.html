Privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026

CheckMyMix is a free macOS app by Sergey Petrachkov. Audio analysis and playback run entirely on your Mac.

Audio and analysis

CheckMyMix reads the audio file you choose and uses its contents and file details to analyze and play it. Access is read-only. The app does not upload audio, filenames, or reports, and the developer does not receive them.

If you choose Generate Interpretation, CheckMyMix uses Apple’s on-device language model to explain a summary of the measurements. The app does not send the summary or explanation to a server.

The current report and interpretation are kept in memory and cleared when you replace the file, close the window, or quit. There is no saved report history. Your original audio file stays where you stored it.

Accounts and tracking

CheckMyMix does not require an account and does not collect personal information through the app. It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

This website

These pages use no forms, analytics, or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical information, such as your IP address, to provide and protect its service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

Support email and deletion requests

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include. This information is used to respond and troubleshoot. Support messages are kept as needed to handle the conversation and any follow-up.

For privacy questions or to request deletion of your support correspondence, email smart.longevity.solutions@gmail.com.
